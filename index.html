Research Paper

Breaking From the Classics? Progressive Education and the Ancient Tradition

Writing 111 · Fall 2026

Classics are disappearing from American schools, and progressive education usually gets the credit (or the blame). In this paper I ask whether progressives like John Dewey really broke from the classical tradition, or whether they reshaped its ideas while claiming to reject them. I argue that what they actually broke from was 19th-century American classicism, like the idea that Latin was mainly "mental discipline." In doing that, they ended up closer to the ancient tradition than the classicists they were replacing.

Rhetorical Paper

Selling the College Experience: A Rhetorical Analysis of a University Homepage

Writing 111 · Fall 2026

A university homepage is one of the first things a future student sees, and every part of it is built to persuade. In this paper I break down how one homepage uses images, word choice and layout to make an argument about who the school is and who belongs there. I look at who the page is really written for, and at what it chooses to show and what it leaves out.
